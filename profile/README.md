# Information Systems Research Group

### Grupo de Investigación en Sistemas de Información

**University of Almería · Universidad de Almería**

Welcome to the GitHub organization of the **Information Systems Research Group**
at the University of Almería (Spain).

We conduct research in Information Systems and related areas, developing
methods, models, software, and other research outcomes.

This organization provides access to our researchers, research projects,
open-source software, datasets, and other research resources.

---

## Research

Our research covers different areas related to Information Systems, including:

- Information Systems
- Software Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Decision Support Systems
- Data and Knowledge Management
- Digital Transformation

> This list can be adapted to reflect the official research lines of the group.
